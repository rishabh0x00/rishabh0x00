# Hi, I’m Rishabh Sharma 👋

## About me

I am a **Blockchain Engineer** with **7+ years of experience** building production-grade systems across **DeFi, protocol infrastructure, wallets, backend systems, and execution layers**. I entered the blockchain space in **2019**, driven by the idea of a truly **permissionless and decentralized financial system**, and since then I’ve worked end-to-end—from smart contracts and distributed backend services to infrastructure and user-facing products operating at real economic scale.

My early work focused on **core blockchain and financial infrastructure**, including **bridges, multisig wallets, DeFi protocols, NFT ecosystems, marketplaces, and derivatives-based AMMs**. I’ve also led security reviews, protocol upgrades, and large-scale contract migrations, including identifying high-impact vulnerabilities and designing safer, more maintainable replacements.

Over time, I moved deeper into **protocol design, backend architecture, and execution systems**. My work includes architecting **shared-security and AVS-style systems**, staking and slashing frameworks, attestation pipelines, cross-chain coordination mechanisms, and transaction execution infrastructure. More recently, I’ve worked extensively on **intent-based execution, MEV-aware systems, account abstraction, and low-latency Rust infrastructure**, building systems that connect off-chain computation with trust-minimized on-chain settlement.

Alongside protocol development, I’ve built **backend and distributed systems** using **Rust, Node.js, and TypeScript**, including APIs, solvers, bundlers, indexers, asynchronous processing pipelines, transaction orchestration services, and real-time blockchain infrastructure. I enjoy working on systems where **backend architecture, cryptography, blockchain state, and high-performance execution** come together.

Security and correctness are a major part of how I approach engineering. My experience includes **internal audits, invariant and fuzz testing, fork simulations, formal-verification collaboration, upgrade-safe architectures, monitoring, and incident-response tooling** using Foundry, Hardhat, OpenZeppelin, Tenderly, and related infrastructure.

Today, I operate at the intersection of **hands-on engineering, architecture, and product ownership**—designing systems, reviewing technical architectures, leading cross-functional engineering efforts, and taking products from an idea through implementation and production. My motivation remains the same as when I started: **build decentralized and financial systems that are powerful under the hood, but simple enough to be useful in the real world.**
